Energy Consumption and Carbon Emissions

Technology is everywhere, and a lot of people have a phone and more than one piece of technology. Most of this technology is stored in a cloud of some type, which uses even more energy to store and, therefore, increases environmental problems and energy consumption and carbon emissions are one of the most significant implications. data gets stored in a physical building that organisations use to keep and run their data which is known as a data centre and these require a huge amount of energy to keep running and according to the International Energy Agency (IEA) data centres account for 1-1.5% of the global electricity use and at this rate is only increasing.

The energy that is used to power these centres often come from non-renewable sources which produces greenhouse gases and organisations continue to collect and process so much unnecessary data as according to to the Observatory of Public Sector Innovation (OPSI) 60% of data in data centres is dark data which uses unnecessary energy therefore increasing the environmental implications.

Electronic Waste

Electronic waste also known as e-waste which is electrical and electronic products such as computers, mobile phones and televisions etc get thrown away as they break and newer upgrades get released therefore creating more e-waste. If they're not removed appropriately it can cause damage in the environment,According to the World Health Organisation in 2022 it had an estimated 62 million tonnes of e-waste was produced worldwide and less than a quarter was correctly disposed of

A number of toxins can be found from e-waste,according to the World Health Organisation it can release up to 1000 different types of toxins and these toxins can be absorbed into the environment and pollute it.One of the worst ways is open burning as it produces toxic fumes therefore contributing to even more greenhouse gas emissions and further polluting the environment.

Physical resources

In our case study we found that in 2022 there was an estimated 333 billion emails sent and received around the world however out of that only a small portion are opened and so more data is just stored away in a cloud unused which uses unnecessary space creating more demand for data space. Which leads to the physical resources needed to build the data centres, as more space gets used up the demand for data storage increases and so bigger data centres need to be built which take up a lot of land, and therefore the land needs to be cleared which can have effects on an ecosystem and lead to the disruption of habitats, pollution and increased carbon emissions. Another concern is the water consumption, as in order to keep stable temperatures data centres use cooling systems including air conditioners, cooling towers and chillers to the pipes. These methods cool it down and releases the heat into the environment.
