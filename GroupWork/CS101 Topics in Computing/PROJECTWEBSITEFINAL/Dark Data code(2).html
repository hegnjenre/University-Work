Positive Impacts

There are also advantages of AI in terms of the environment, AI can help to manage and recycle e-waste (Admin,.2024), e-waste has grown to be an enormous environmental concern. AI can automate the processes involved in the recycling of e-waste, simplifying the sorting and dismantling of electronic devices (Admin,.2024). Advanced algorithms within AI are consistently being trained; these can instantly and more accurately identify and sort various components of electronics, therefore reducing time and labor due to manual sorting (Admin,.2024).

Another advantage of AI is that we can use it to help combat other environmental issues e.g., AI can significantly enhance our ability to monitor environmental changes, particularly by tracking where icebergs are melting and at what rate (Stephanie Safdie, 2024). In fact, AI can perform this task 10,000 times faster than humans alone, making it a powerful tool in the fight against climate change and its effects on the environment (Stephanie Safdie, 2024). AI can further help in the running of day-to-day business operation through correctly managing various business processes(Stephanie Safdie, 2024). It can track and generate different types of algorithms because it can monitor all business operations at once, It can do all this to a much higher standard and precision than a human, hence reducing the risk of mistakes. (Stephanie Safdie,. 2024)

Summary

To sum up, AI enables a wide range of benefits and enhancements to many industries; however, the environmental impact of AI cannot be continually overlooked. Energy consumption, water wastage, carbon emission and CO2 production are just some of the environmental issues associated with AI systems. As AI further develops itself, researchers and companies should adopt the sustainability process that will help reduce the environmental destruction of AI. By going with a more energy-friendly model and trying to utilize renewable sources of energy, the negative impact which AI has on the environment will be reduced, and the developments made in this field will come without the sacrifice of the planet
